TRI-CASS COMPANION

USB / portable kit — your offline AI on a stick

Three taps

  1. This folder is already on your USB (or copy the whole unzipped kit).
  2. Windows: open Windows\Start-Tri-Cass.bat
    Mac: open Mac/Start-Tri-Cass.command (right‑click → Open the first time).
  3. Pick a local model in Chat (Ollama on the host PC) and go.
Start on Windows Start on Mac

Travels with the drive

Settings, Vault, saved chats, Knowledge/, and Files/ stay on this USB. Unplug, move PCs, plug in again — same personal setup. Heavier than the bootable OS images, same Companion features.

Add your own wiki / folders

Drop notes into Knowledge/, or in the app use Add from this PC to copy folders from the host drive. Chat → Save chat writes into Chats/.
